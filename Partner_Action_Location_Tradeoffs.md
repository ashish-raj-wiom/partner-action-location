# Partner Action Location — Tradeoffs Register

Companion to `Partner_Action_Location_PRD.md` (v2.1). Not part of the PRD. This is the PM's record of every decision taken while writing it — what was chosen, what was rejected, and why — so the reasoning survives outside the spec.

Interview and drafting: 29–31 July 2026. Owner: Ashish Raj.

| # | Decision point | Chosen | Rejected options | Why (PM's stated reason) | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What the feature is for | Understand where each partner role performs its work | Building it primarily as a fraud-evidence system | "Objective is to understand the partner's patterns from where he is doing the actions." | 29 Jul |
| 2 | Comparing a location against a reference point — the office-attribution use case in the original brief | Out of scope. The spec captures where actions happened and defines no comparison | Keeping a premises-proximity measurement question and a distance threshold | "There needs no source of partner premises for this spec. The spec aims to capture location on csp user actions, thats all." | 30 Jul |
| 3 | Behaviour when no location is available | Record the action with a status explaining why; never block, delay or degrade it | Blocking the action until a reading is obtained | Blocking would hand every partner a way to stall their own task. | 29 Jul |
| 4 | Where the data is stored | Not specified — the implementer decides. No store, platform or transport is named | Naming CleverTap as the destination (chosen 29 Jul, reversed 30 Jul); writing to a backend store | 29 Jul: "Clevertap Only… everything is handled via that." 30 Jul, reversed: "Do not explicitly mention that this needs to be sent on Clevertap… How it's going to be done is something the tech has to decide." | 30 Jul |
| 5 | Scope | Every action a partner user takes in either app, every service, screen opens and taps included | Installation flow only, as originally briefed | "The scope has changed now, it not only covers installation but it covers every service, every action the CSP users take." | 29 Jul |
| 6 | Analysis granularity | Per individual — named user, named booking, named action | Aggregate-only with a purpose limitation | "Yes, per individual analysis." Written in from v1 deliberately, so Legal reviews the wider purpose once rather than re-reviewing later as happened with the Call Logs PRD. | 29 Jul |
| 7 | Whether location alone can justify a consequence against a person | **No.** It may direct an investigation; the consequence needs other evidence (R5b) | Permitting payout, posture, access or disciplinary action on location alone | Approved as a MUST NOT and, at the time, a zero-tolerance guardrail — the guardrail was later removed, see decision 24. Follows the Call Logs precedent, which made partner enforcement a MUST NOT. | 30 Jul |
| 8 | What is recorded alongside the coordinate | Accuracy radius and mock-location flag | Latitude and longitude alone | Accepted that bare coordinates cannot distinguish a 10 m fix from a 3 km one, or a measured reading from a fabricated one — and that per-individual conclusions on such data are not defensible. | 29 Jul |
| 9 | Reading currency (age) | Out of the spec. The requirement is to fetch the location when the action happens; how current the underlying reading is, is Engineering's concern | Recording an age in seconds, with a configurable fresh/stale boundary (chosen 29 Jul, removed 30 Jul) | "Age is something tech has to figure out how to do. My requirement is fetch the location when the action happens." Noted at the time: an offline-queued record can no longer distinguish a fresh reading from a cached one. | 30 Jul |
| 10 | Mock-location flag | Keep | Dropping it | "Mock flag is fine." It is the only field separating a measured coordinate from a fabricated one, and it costs nothing — Android already reports it per reading. | 30 Jul |
| 11 | Making location capture compulsory | No mechanism in this spec. Both apps already require location permission and both re-ask if it is withdrawn | An app-launch gate behind a per-app feature flag, defaulting off (chosen 29 Jul, withdrawn 30 Jul) | "Location permission is mandatory today. Without it, the app does not work… No need for that flag now." Verified: the CSP app blocks all use via `PermissionFlow`; the Technician app gates on the login screen via `feature/auth/PermissionFlowHost`. | 30 Jul |
| 12 | Permission mechanics in general | Not the PRD's business. Only the assumption that permission stays compulsory is stated | A rule prohibiting background location (written 29 Jul, withdrawn 30 Jul) | "This is a tech call, not PM's." The rule was also factually wrong — the CSP app already declares `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`. | 30 Jul |
| 13 | Target accuracy | 20 m default; range delegated to Engineering (C-01, OV-5) | A PM-set range; no target at all | "As accurate as possible without hampering the app latency. Tech has to finalise the range." Recorded as a target, not a filter: a reading the device can only place within 500 m is still recorded, with 500 m reported. | 30 Jul |
| 14 | Retention | Not a PRD parameter. An open question for Engineering, with the PM's access requirement written into R5a | A PM-set retention period (C-05, deleted) | "Put as an open question for engineering. My usecase is, I should be able to access it for my analysis." | 30 Jul |
| 15 | Success metrics | Partner users with location on at least one action; share of a user's actions carrying location. No baseline or target until production data | High-confidence coverage percentages, an "analysable user" threshold (C-04), and a role-population metric | "No baseline exists for these. Will put when we see some data post prod release." | 30 Jul |
| 16 | Identifying whose location it is | An identifier that is unique, stable and never reused, plus the CSP and the role, on every record — attributed to whoever was signed in at the instant of the action (R6, G4) | Leaving identity implicit in whatever the analytics layer already carries | "One thing which is crucial is, I should be able to identify the user correctly for whom I am seeing the location data. I should know who is the user very concretly." Made a zero-tolerance invariant because attribution either holds absolutely or the data cannot name anyone — which is what decision 7 rests on. | 30 Jul |
| 17 | Screens | None added or changed. The permission flows and the device-location-off prompt all already exist | Specifying a launch gate, an ask sheet and a device-location-off prompt | "It is currently present in the app, no new design is required." | 30 Jul |
| 18 | Regression scope | Every task family (INSTALL, RESTORE, RECHARGE, NETBOX_PICKUP, OUTAGE, SHIFTING), every flow carrying no task, and every failure path | Regression against the installation flow only | "It just talks about installation flow, It should also talk about all the flows working as expected." | 30 Jul |
| 19 | Recording a coordinate on an action whose status says none was available | No rule forbidding it | A MUST NOT preventing a cached coordinate being attached to a status-only action | "Remove." Noted at the time: a record can now carry a coordinate while its own status says no reading was available. MQ-3 will surface it in the data if it matters. | 30 Jul |
| 20 | How the document reads | State requirements only. No commentary on what was deliberately left out or removed | Boundary and header notes explaining what the spec does not name and why | "Do not put things in the PRD which are removed on purpose. Saying, this is removed fails the whole purpose." | 30 Jul |
| 21 | Whether analysis is a requirement or an assumption | **A requirement.** R5c obliges the data to sit where the PM can query it directly, grouping and filtering freely, with no engineering request per question; R5d keeps every record tied to the action it belongs to | Leaving "permit analysis" to mean an engineer runs queries on request | "Since the purpose of this prd will be fulfilled by analytics, have you covered the acceptance criteria that I should be able to write SQL queries on the data stored… Where is How — engineering has to decide." Without R5d the data would be people and coordinates with no way to tell an install submission from a wallet screen open. | 31 Jul |
| 22 | Coverage of actions added after launch | **In scope.** R1c obliges every action defined in future to carry the same data from its first release, with a MUST NOT against shipping one without it | Covering only the actions that exist today | "Any action which is defined and will be defined in future should capture lat long." Without it a new event could ship six months later with no coordinate and violate nothing. | 31 Jul |
| 23 | Naming CleverTap at all | **Named once**, in the §8 Action definition, as the stream that already reports these interactions with event properties and timestamps | Leaving the action set undefined; or naming CleverTap as the location destination (removed on 30 Jul, decision 4) | "Also mention that these actions are already reported in Clevertap today with proper event properties, timestamps etc." Current-state fact about the action set, not a destination for the location data — the two must not be conflated on a later edit. | 31 Jul |
| 24 | Monitoring and guarding the use of the data | **Neither.** MQ-5 and the "no consequence without corroboration" guardrail are both removed | Keeping a measurement question that nothing in the system could answer, and a guardrail resting on it | "Remove MQ-5… Remove G4 as well." The evidence behind a consequence is held by whoever applies it, not by anything this spec builds. R5b survives as a rule and now also requires the evidence to be recorded, so its acceptance criterion tests something real. | 31 Jul |
| 25 | Slot proposal in the acceptance criteria | Replaced with actions that exist today — mark arrived, assign a technician | Keeping slot-proposal examples | "CSP do not propose slot anymore to the customer." Verified: `PROPOSE_SLOT` survives as a constant but now sits beside `CUSTOMER_PROPOSED_SLOT_CONFIRM` and `HOLD_FOR_CUSTOMER_SCHEDULE`, so scheduling has moved to the customer. | 31 Jul |

## Open questions at the time of writing

Held in the PRD's own **Open questions** section (OV-7), not here: retention period and the C-01 range are Engineering's; whether the identifier alone suffices without a name or mobile number is the PM's; and the Reviewer plus the Legal/DPDP, Android and Analytics consulted names are outstanding.

## Corrections made during drafting, for the record

Three claims were written into the spec and later found wrong against the app code. Each was corrected before sign-off, and each is why the assumption in §1 is stated explicitly rather than assumed:

- The premise that CleverTap already carried latitude and longitude on every event. It does not — neither app obtains a location reading at all.
- A rule prohibiting background location. The CSP app already declares `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`.
- That the Technician app had no launch-time permission requirement. It has one, in `feature/auth/PermissionFlowHost`.
